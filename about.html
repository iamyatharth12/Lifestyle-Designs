Home/About

A small studio, obsessed with craft.

We started in 2014 with a single screen-printing table, a borrowed embroidery machine, and a list of friends who needed custom tees. A decade later — same obsession, slightly larger studio.

Our beginning

From a garage in Indiranagar to studios across India.

Lifestyle Designs was founded by two friends — a graphic designer and a textile engineer — who believed India deserved a customization studio that took both craft and design seriously. We opened our first 200 sq. ft. workshop in 2014 with a single promise: every piece will be made as if our name is stitched into it.

A decade later, we've outgrown two studios and now operate from a 6,000 sq. ft. atelier with an in-house team of designers, printers, embroiderers and tailors. We've shipped to 18 countries. But our promise hasn't changed.

Our craft

In-house, end-to-end, obsessively.

Design, sampling, screen printing, DTG, embroidery, stitching, finishing, packing — every step happens under our roof. We don't outsource production, and we don't hide behind a sales team. When you work with us, you work with the people making your product.

  • 22-member team across design, production and finishing
  • 8,000 stitches-per-minute embroidery machines
  • Pantone-certified colour matching on every print
  • Three-stage quality control on every order
