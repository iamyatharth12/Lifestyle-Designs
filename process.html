Home/Custom process

A studio process, end to end.

Four deliberate stages — designed for clarity on your side and craftsmanship on ours. No factories, no middlemen. Just a small team obsessed with getting every detail right.

01
Step one

Share Your Idea

It starts with a conversation. Tell us about your brand, your people, your event — and the impression you want to leave. The more context, the better. We listen first.

  • Discovery call or studio visit
  • Brand moodboard & reference review
  • Quantity, timeline & budget scoping
  • Written brief within 24 hours
02
Step two

Design & Personalize

Our designers translate your brief into mockups — choosing fabrics, fits, prints and embroidery that express your identity. You'll see digital proofs, physical samples, and Pantone references before we produce a single unit.

  • Digital mockups & tech-packs
  • Fabric swatches & Pantone matching
  • Sample garment for your sign-off
  • Unlimited revisions until it feels right
03
Step three

Craft & Produce

Once approved, production begins — under one roof. Our screen printers, embroidery operators and tailors work side-by-side, allowing constant quality checks. Every piece is inspected before it leaves the studio.

  • In-house screen printing & DTG
  • Computerised embroidery — 8,000 SPM
  • Three-stage quality control
  • Real-time progress updates
04
Step four

Delivered With Pride

Each garment is hand-folded, individually packed when required, and delivered to your door, your office, or directly to your event venue — on the date we promised, every time.

  • Hand-folded, branded packaging
  • Doorstep, office or venue delivery
  • Pan-India & international shipping
  • Re-order discounts for repeat clients
Frequently asked

A few honest answers.

What is the minimum order quantity?

+

For most collections it's 25 pieces. For personalised gifts and single pieces, we offer no-minimum options.

How long does a typical order take?

+

Standard turnaround is 7–10 working days after sample approval. Rush delivery (48–72 hours) is available for most collections.

Can I see a sample before placing a bulk order?

+

Absolutely. We always produce a sample garment (or digital proof for printed items) and ship it to you for sign-off before bulk production.

Do you ship internationally?

+

Yes — we've shipped to over 18 countries via trusted logistics partners. We handle all customs documentation.
